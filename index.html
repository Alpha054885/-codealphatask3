Q: Why use shutil.move instead of os.rename?

            
A: shutil.move works reliably across different disk partitions or mount points, while os.rename throws an EXDEV error if partitions differ.

          

          

            
Q: How does duplicate collision prevention work?

            
A: get_unique_destination_path() checks os.path.exists() and dynamically appends an incrementing counter (_1, _2) before moving.

          

          

            
Q: How are subdirectories handled?

            
A: The script uses os.path.isfile() to strictly process regular files, keeping subfolders untouched.
